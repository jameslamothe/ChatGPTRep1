# Recording and acceptance checklist

## Exact pilot recording script — draft pending Thai-speaker confirmation

Ask a native Thai speaker to confirm each conventional name and word before recording. Record the name and word as separate takes, in a natural educational speaking voice, without an English introduction, music, announcements or spoken filenames. A consistent speaker and microphone are preferred. A male voice is a preference, not a reason to choose unclear or improperly licensed speech.

| Letter | Clip A: full letter name | Clip B: example word |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ก | กอ ไก่ | ไก่ |
| ข | ขอ ไข่ | ไข่ |
| ค | คอ ควาย | ควาย |
| ง | งอ งู | งู |

| ป | ปอ ปลา | ปลา |

The full name and example word overlap intentionally. Do not extract the example by cutting a name recording unless the license permits editing and a Thai reviewer confirms the resulting word sounds complete and natural. Do not call a spoken letter name an isolated consonant sound.

## Rights gate — before downloading for use or hosting

For each actual candidate, retain:

1. Original source page, direct file URL and creator/speaker credit as published. Leave unknown identities unknown.
2. Explicit license name/version, license URL and file-specific evidence that the license applies to this recording. Public listening access, attribution alone and the educational purpose of the app do not supply permission.
3. Confirmation that public web hosting and redistribution are permitted. Check attribution, share-alike, noncommercial, no-derivatives and other conditions against the planned use. Do not assume a site's text license covers embedded audio.
4. Whether trimming, volume normalization, conversion and other planned modifications are permitted, plus a record of actual modifications. Keep the original license evidence and required notices.
5. For commissioned recordings, written permission covering public hosting, redistribution, needed modifications and the agreed speaker credit. Do not contact or publish recordings on the speaker's behalf without authorization.

Do not download a file with unclear rights as an app asset or hotlink it as a workaround. Do not infer native-speaker identity from a language label alone.

## Linguistic and listening gate

Record reviewer identity (with permission), review date and outcome. A competent native Thai speaker or qualified Thai teacher should check:

- Correct letter/name/word association; natural Standard Thai pronunciation.
- Tones and vowel lengths in every syllable; correct /pl/ in ปลา and both syllables of ใบไม้.
- ก versus ข/ค aspiration; จ versus ช aspiration; ด versus ต; บ versus ป; initial ง.
- Complete, natural starts and endings; no extra English sounds or spelling readouts.
- Understandable pacing without unnatural exaggeration.

Listen to all twenty actual selected clips. Metadata, filenames, automatic tests and file decodability cannot establish pronunciation correctness.

## Technical quality gate

Check clean speech, no clipping, low background noise, minimal excess silence, comparable perceived volume and a consistent voice where practical. Keep a lossless original if legitimately supplied. Choose a browser-supported distribution format only after confirming conversion rights. Record a hash of the final selected file so later changes can be detected.

For future playback implementation: verify the selected clip plays from a user tap in Safari on iPhone and Chrome on Android, with no overlapping clips or playback continuing after leaving a card. Test missing-file and decode errors. This is a future acceptance checklist; no audio was downloaded or tested in this sourcing task.
